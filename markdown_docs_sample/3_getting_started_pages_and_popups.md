# Pages And Popups

Pages and Popups can be found in the left menu of your site editor. The Page area allows you to define the organization of your website by creating and re-ordering pages. The Popups area allows you to add popups to a specific page; this can draw attention to important information without pulling visitors away from the area they are on.

## Pages

Note: Each site can have a maximum of 1,000 pages. There is a limit of 1,500 blog posts, but dynamic pages have no hard limit.

To manage this, click Pages in the left menu. You can add a new page by clicking “Add Page.”

![image](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5ada36872c7d3a0e93678b41/images/66a917b26eb51e63b8f9e089/file-IVsk9v8A1Z.png)

This will bring up the page options. Select a blank page or the relevant type, which will come equipped with associated widgets.

Note: the “Page URL” type links to another page URL and doesn’t create a new page.

![image](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5ada36872c7d3a0e93678b41/images/66b4dfad6eb51e63b8f9e6a8/file-iAgGFyYMmm.png)

Once you click on your desired page type, enter your “Page Name” and click “+ Add Page.” This will add your new page to the bottom of the list.

![image](https://app.wocode.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/pagesandpopups-3.jpg)

## Page Builder:

The page builder feature lets you effortlessly create and design pages using pre-designed sections. Once you’ve built a page, you can instantly add it to your site by clicking the orange “Add Page to Site” button. Alternatively, if you’re not ready to publish, you can save your work by clicking “Save for Later” which stores the page under the “Pages in Progress” section in the pages panel.

![image](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5ada36872c7d3a0e93678b41/images/66b4f1fc493fd0116031ed3c/file-ZIELpCBaUi.png)

Pages saved in the “Pages in Progress” section won’t appear on your site until you’re ready. To finalize, simply click the ellipses, choose “Continue with Page Builder,” and then select “Add Page to Site” to publish your page.

![image](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5ada36872c7d3a0e93678b41/images/66b4e18c067e9c553dc82488/file-8DOMrrcYeq.png)

You can reorganize the pages by clicking and dragging as desired. To nest a page within another, click and drag slightly to the right underneath another page.

![image](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5ada36872c7d3a0e93678b41/images/66a9184567ad674af17cb891/file-9qUxPCy7CV.png)

To edit page settings, click on the gear icon next to any individual page. This will give you multiple options.

![image](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5ada36872c7d3a0e93678b41/images/66a9186848915f570c6a8c20/file-ZQHA1gCjx7.png)

## Popups

To add a popup, click the Pages section, select “Popups,” and click “New Popup.”

![image](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5ada36872c7d3a0e93678b41/images/66bce96b6eb51e63b8f9ef7c/file-RKgzRhYVFB.png)

This will bring up the popup options. Select a blank popup or the relevant type, which will come equipped with associated widgets.

![image](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5ada36872c7d3a0e93678b41/images/66bce99c65e01777a9333d64/file-JQO83cJaWA.png)

Once you click on your desired popup type, enter your “Popup Name” and click “+ Add Popup.” This will add your new popup to the site.

![image](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5ada36872c7d3a0e93678b41/images/66bcea198919a15d1b1d31a0/file-YobUf4031u.png)

In order to activate the popup, you must use a link/button or define a personalization rule.

![image](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5ada36872c7d3a0e93678b41/images/66bcea506eb51e63b8f9ef7e/file-QCqrlJn2IK.png)

Many widgets give you the option to link to a popup, including buttons, text, and Photo Gallery image links. From the individual widget content tab, click “Popup” and select the relevant popup.

![image](https://app.wocode.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/pagesandpopups-10.jpg)

To create a condition for a popup, you can create a rule by selecting Personalize from the left menu. Define the trigger(s) and select “Popup” as the action.

View more about Personalization Rules.

Note: popups created with personalization rules will only appear once per session.

![image](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/5ada36872c7d3a0e93678b41/images/66bceaec8919a15d1b1d31a3/file-zkZUFRx27F.png)

You can edit rows, columns, and add widgets to any popup and you can adjust the size of the popup by dragging the corner. Click on the popup to bring up the content and design tabs, which give you multiple customization options.

![image](https://app.wocode.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/pagesandpopups-12.jpg)
